About CADIG

A deeper view of hockey talent.

CADIG Scouting Agency is an independent hockey scouting and analytics organization focused on player evaluation, development pathways, league intelligence and career outcomes.

Our approach combines traditional scouting, video assessment, statistical analysis and historical player-development research to create structured, repeatable evaluations.

The objective is not only to understand what a player has produced, but how the player plays, the level and environment in which that performance occurred, and where that skill set may translate next.

What We Do

Evaluation beyond the scoresheet.

CADIG combines observation and structured evidence across four connected areas of hockey evaluation.

01

Player Scouting

Live and video evaluation focused on skating, hockey intelligence, puck skills, offensive impact, defensive reliability, competitiveness and long-term projection.

02

Performance Analytics

Player, team and league analysis designed to contextualize production, identify trends and support scouting observations with repeatable evidence.

03

Development Pathways

Historical player movement and advancement research used to understand where players have come from, which environments develop talent and what next steps may be realistic.

04

Organizational Intelligence

League comparison, recruiting intelligence, alumni outcomes and development benchmarking designed to support broader hockey decisions.

From observation to projection.

Hockey Intelligence

One model. Multiple layers of context.

CADIG is developing a standardized hockey intelligence model that compares players, leagues, development pathways and career outcomes across different hockey systems.

01

Career Outcome

Track the highest competitive level reached across a player's complete career history.

02

Development Pathway

Evaluate the strength of a league or environment as a route toward future advancement.

03

Advancement

Measure upward, lateral and downward player movement across competitive levels.

04

Cross-League Context

Standardize league identities and competition levels so players can be compared across systems.

CADIG Framework

From data collection to hockey decisions.

The model is designed to support scouting reports, recruiting intelligence, organizational benchmarking and cross-league analysis.

Scouting Reports Current league context, comparable level, projected destination, advancement projection and scout grades.
Recruiting Intelligence Feeder leagues, regions, age groups and player profiles associated with stronger outcomes.
Organizational Benchmarking Compare alumni outcomes, development value and roster-source effectiveness.
League Intelligence Standardized league identities, competition tiers, pathway scores and historical player movement.
