What We Do

Scouting and intelligence from the player level to the league level.

Our work is structured around two core report types: broad statistical intelligence and deeper individual scouting evaluation.

REPORT 01

League & Statistical Intelligence

Large-scale league reports organize player production, age, position, team, league and developmental information into a usable scouting reference.

REPORT 02

Individual Player Scouting

Focused player reports combine live or video-based observation with statistical context, strengths, areas for development, role assessment and projection.

INTELLIGENCE

Development Pathway Research

Mapping the progression of players through youth, junior, prep, college and international hockey systems to understand developmental environments and opportunities.

ANALYTICS

Comparative Player Analysis

Statistical and contextual comparisons help identify players performing above their environment, comparable development profiles and potential scouting targets.

Scouting Process

From identification to projection.

A repeatable process helps move from a large player pool toward focused individual evaluation.

01

Identify

Statistical research and league monitoring establish the initial player pool.

02

Observe

Players are evaluated through live viewing, video and structured scouting criteria.

03

Contextualize

Performance is compared against age, league quality, role and developmental environment.

04

Project

Current ability is translated into developmental upside and potential future role.
