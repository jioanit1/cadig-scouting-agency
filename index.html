About CADIG

Building a deeper hockey intelligence network.

CADIG Scouting Agency is an independent hockey scouting and analytics organization focused on player evaluation, development pathways and league intelligence.

Our objective is to combine the judgment of traditional scouting with structured data and repeatable analytical methods—creating evaluations that consider both what happens on the ice and the environment in which it happens.
