About CADIG

A deeper view of hockey talent.

CADIG Scouting Agency is an independent hockey scouting and intelligence organization focused on player evaluation, development pathways, league intelligence and career outcomes.

Our approach combines traditional scouting, video assessment, statistical analysis and historical player-development research.

The goal is to understand not only what a player has produced, but how the player plays, the competitive environment in which that production occurred and where that skill set may translate next.

CADIG's broader intelligence work connects player evaluation to league context, advancement history and developmental pathways.

What We Do

Evaluation beyond the scoresheet.

CADIG combines observation and structured evidence across connected areas of hockey evaluation.

01

Player Scouting

Live and video evaluation focused on skating, hockey intelligence, puck skills, offensive impact, defensive reliability, competitiveness and projection.

02

Performance Analytics

Player, team and league analysis designed to contextualize production, identify trends and support scouting observations with repeatable evidence.

03

Development Pathways

Historical player movement and advancement research used to understand where players have come from and which environments develop talent.

04

League Intelligence

Cross-league comparison, player movement, development context and historical career outcomes.
