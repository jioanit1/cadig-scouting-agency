About CADIG

A deeper view of hockey talent.

CADIG Scouting Agency is an independent hockey scouting and analytics organization focused on player evaluation, development pathways and league intelligence.

We combine structured scouting observations with statistical context and development research to build more complete player evaluations.

The objective is simple: understand not only what a player has done, but how the player plays, the environment in which that performance occurred and where the player's skill set may translate next.

What We Do

A complete evaluation approach.

01

Player Scouting

Live and video evaluation using a standardized framework covering skating, hockey intelligence, puck skills, offensive impact, defensive reliability, compete level and long-term projection.

02

Performance Analytics

Player, team and league statistical analysis designed to put production into context, identify trends and support traditional scouting observations.

03

Development Pathways

Historical player movement, league strength and advancement research used to understand where players have come from and what competitive environments may represent realistic next steps.

04

League Intelligence

Structured analysis of leagues, teams, competition levels and player outcomes to create a broader understanding of the hockey development landscape.

Traditional hockey evaluation meets structured intelligence.
