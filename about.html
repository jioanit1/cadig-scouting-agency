CADIG is an independent hockey scouting and intelligence organization focused on player evaluation, development pathways, league intelligence and career outcomes.

OUR APPROACH

A deeper view of hockey talent.

Traditional scouting remains central. CADIG adds structured analytical and developmental context to help explain how a player plays, the environment in which production occurred, and how that skill set may translate.

The goal is not to replace hockey judgment with numbers. It is to connect observation, evidence and development history into a clearer evaluation process.

“See the player. Understand the environment. Follow the pathway. Project what comes next.”

Independent

Evaluation designed around evidence and clearly stated observations.

Structured

A consistent framework makes reports more comparable across viewings.

Development-focused

Projection includes pathway and environment, not only current results.
