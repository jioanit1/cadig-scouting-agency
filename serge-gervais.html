TEAM / SERGE GERVAIS

SERGE GERVAIS

Co-Founder & Director of Scouting

Hockey has been part of Serge’s life for more than 40 years as a player, coach, manager, league administrator and scout.

His evaluation approach looks beyond statistics to understand the complete player — hockey IQ, competitiveness, character, decision-making, consistency and development potential.

Serge focuses on what a player can become, not simply what they are today.
