PLAYER EVALUATION

What we evaluate.

The framework is built around traits that can translate across roles, teams and levels of competition.

Skating

Acceleration, speed, edges, mobility, balance and functional pace.

Hockey Intelligence

Reads, anticipation, spacing, decision-making and adaptability.

Puck Skills

Handling, passing, receiving, deception, shooting and execution under pressure.

Compete

Engagement, motor, physical willingness, second effort and consistency.

Offensive Impact

Creation, transition, playmaking, finishing and off-puck contribution.

Defensive Impact

Positioning, stick detail, tracking, retrievals and transition defense.

Role & Usage

Special teams, deployment, teammates, opposition and situational responsibility.

Projection

Transferable tools, development priorities, risk and possible next level.

METHODOLOGY
