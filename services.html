SERVICE PRINCIPLE

Evidence before advice.

Each service should be grounded in a documented observation, a defined dataset or a transparent analytical framework.

01

Define the question

What decision is the scouting or intelligence work trying to support?

02

Collect evidence

Game observations, video, public data, pathway histories and contextual information.

03

Evaluate

Use repeatable criteria and clearly distinguish fact, observation and projection.

04

Deliver

Report, profile, dashboard or research memo matched to the decision.
